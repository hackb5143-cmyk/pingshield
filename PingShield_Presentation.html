PROBLEM STATEMENT • CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES

Why Traditional Tags & Emergency Outreach Fail

02 / 09
PRIVACY EXPOSURE
Severe Privacy & Doxxing Risks
Pasting phone numbers on bags, bikes, or laptops exposes owners directly to unsolicited spam, cyber stalking, extortion, and social engineering.
THE HARSH REALITY
100% of plain tags leak personal contact records to any passerby without consent or access control.
GOLDEN HOUR LOSS
Delayed Road Emergency Help
During vehicle crashes or medical crises, willing bystanders cannot reach family members because the victim's phone is locked behind PINs and biometrics.
THE HARSH REALITY
Critical golden-hour trauma response is paralyzed because first responders have zero verified contact vectors.
HIGH ABANDONMENT
Severe App & Registration Friction
Finders will not download a 50MB mobile app, create an account, or surrender their personal phone number just to do a quick good deed and report a lost item.
THE HARSH REALITY
Over 85% drop-off rate occurs when users are forced to install an application to complete a 10-second action.

CORE INNOVATION • ESCALATION & INTEGRITY

The "Two-Attempt" Emergency Escalation Rule

04 / 09
OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW
How the Protocol Operates
STEP 1 • EMERGENCY MODE TRIGGER
Finder switches the scan page to Emergency Mode (e.g. vehicle crash or medical distress).
STEP 2 • OWNER PRIORITY CALL (FAMILY LOCKED)
Owner masked call button unlocks immediately. Family emergency contacts remain strictly LOCKED to prevent false alarms or panicking relatives.
STEP 3 • SERVER-VERIFIED ESCALATION
Only after 2 server-verified unanswered calls to the owner does the "Call Masked Family Contact" button unlock!
CRYPTOGRAPHIC DEFENSE
Anti-Bypass Security Architecture
SERVER-ENFORCED TELEPHONY CALLBACKS
Escalation is not driven by client clicks. Cloud PBX webhooks transmit signed call outcomes ('no-answer', 'busy', duration) directly to backend workers.
SESSION TIMERS & RATE-LIMITED TOKENS
Backend maintains authenticated scan sessions with bounded expiration windows. Rapid-fire redialing or automated click spoofing is rejected.
TAMPER-PROOF CLIENT INTEGRITY
Refreshing the tab, modifying LocalStorage, or manipulating DOM elements cannot unlock the contact. The API dispenses family tokens only upon verified escalation.

SAFETY GUARDRAILS • FRAUD & COST MITIGATION

Built for Real-World Chaos, Fraud & Privacy Defense

06 / 09
RATE LIMITING & QUOTAS
Anti-Spam Scan & Alert Throttling
Enforces a maximum of 3 ordinary scan alerts per 5 minutes per IP and session token. Daily SMS and telephony quotas completely eliminate runaway cloud provider bills and denial-of-wallet attacks.
EMERGENCY CHANNELING
Distinct Emergency Priority Routing
Verified emergency mode reports safely bypass ordinary spam limits through authenticated session tokens, ensuring genuine highway road accident alerts are never throttled or delayed.
CONSENT & REVOCATION
Explicit Opt-In & One-Tap Revocation
Emergency contacts must explicitly accept a digital invite before being linked to an item. Any contact can revoke consent or permanently block misuse with a single tap at any time.
SYSTEM INTEGRITY
Honest UI & Zero False Promises
Presents honest, real-time UI statuses (Queued, Sent, Delivered, Answered). PingShield never makes misleading claims of automated ambulance dispatch—it transparently empowers human response.
